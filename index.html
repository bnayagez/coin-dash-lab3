LAB 3 · STARTER GAME

Coin Dash

Collect yellow coins. Dodge red drones. How many can you collect in 60 seconds?

How to play

You are the mint-green square. Collect as many yellow coins as you can before the 60-second timer runs out!

Move your player
Use the ↑ ↓ ← → arrow keys or W (up), A (left), S (down), D (right).
Collect coins & score points
Touch a yellow coin to earn 1 point. A new coin appears each time you collect one. Aim for your best score!
Dodge the drones
The red squares are moving enemies. Touching any drone ends your round immediately. Keep your distance!
Try again
Press R at any time to start a fresh round with a new timer and a score of zero.
